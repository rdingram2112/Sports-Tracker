Shot chart

Rankings are ESPN's for each game: the College Football Playoff rankings once they're released, and the AP Top 25 before that, so past weeks show each team's ranking at the time. Top 25 shows games with at least one ranked team; a conference shows games involving any of its teams. Records, rankings and betting lines (spread, over/under and moneyline, usually DraftKings via ESPN) are refreshed each morning and mid-afternoon Eastern, so they're snapshots, not live odds.

Opponent-adjusted EPA judges each game against the strength of that opponent: a team's offensive EPA in a game is compared with what that opponent's defense usually allows (and its defense with what the opponent's offense usually gains), with home field accounted for. Opponent strength is measured from every game played so far, then your "Stats from" choice picks which of a team's games count, so you can see adjusted numbers for the last few games or conference play. Numbers are on the same scale as raw EPA: what the team did against an average FBS opponent.

Success rate is the share of plays that gain at least 50% of the yards to go on 1st down, 70% on 2nd and 100% on 3rd or 4th (touchdowns always count; turnovers never). Early downs are 1st and 2nd, late downs 3rd and 4th. Standard downs are 1st down, 2nd and 7 or less, and 3rd/4th and 4 or less; everything else is a passing down. Sack rate is sacks per dropback, and havoc is the share of plays with a tackle for loss, sack, interception, fumble or pass broken up. Pressure rate counts sacks plus dropbacks the stadium's stat crew noted as hurried or hit, so it undercounts real pressure and varies by crew; it's best for comparing teams within a conference. All of these come from CollegeFootballData.com play-by-play with garbage time excluded, and the Raw / Opponent-adjusted switch applies to them like EPA.

Team stats lists every FBS team with each stat's national rank underneath (green: top 25, red: bottom 25). Points & yards shows points and total yards gained and allowed per game; EPA offense and EPA defense show expected points added per play, per dropback and per rush, gained and allowed. "Stats from" applies to both Team stats and Matchups.

On Matchups, team stats are per game: PTS/G and OPP/G (points scored and allowed) and YDS/G and OPP YDS (total yards gained and allowed), with each team's rank among FBS teams. They count completed games before the selected week (or all games so far for future weeks), limited by "Stats from": the whole season or each team's last 3 or 5 games, and optionally conference games only. The shaded side is better. EPA (expected points added) comes from CollegeFootballData.com with garbage time excluded: OFF is the team's offense and DEF what its defense allows (lower is better), per play, per dropback (DB: passes and sacks) and per rush. QB scrambles can't be separated from designed runs in public play-by-play, so they count as rushes. Past games show the last betting line saved before kickoff when one was captured; ESPN removes lines once games start. Kickoff times are in your local time zone.

Scores come straight from ESPN and refresh every 30 seconds while this view is open (it pauses when the tab is in the background). Times are in your local time zone.

Time periods count back from the most recent game day: last week is the last 7 days, last 2 weeks the last 14, and last month the last 30. Per game lists only players who played in at least half of their team's games in the period, so players who have been out injured drop off; Totals lists everyone who played. Click a column to sort by it, and click again to reverse.

The small number under each stat is the player's percentile among players at the same position (guards, forwards or centers) in the same period and mode: 90th means better than 90% of them. Green is 80th and up, red 20th and below. For turnovers, a high percentile means fewer turnovers. Players below the shooting minimums below get no percentile for that stat.

Shooting stats are totaled across the period: eFG% = (FGM + 0.5 × 3PM) ÷ FGA, 3P% = 3PM ÷ 3PA, 3P rate = 3PA ÷ FGA, FT rate = FTA ÷ FGA (how often a player gets to the line), and FT% = FTM ÷ FTA. When sorting by one of them, low-volume players go after everyone else: under 5 shots a game for eFG%, 3P rate and FT rate, and under 2 attempts a game for 3P% and FT%.

Team is the player's team in his most recent game in the period. Photos and box scores come from ESPN, updated every morning.

Click a team in Team stats to see each game in the selected window, most recent first.

Each stat shows the team's value with its league rank in parentheses for the selected window. Rank 1 is best: highest net rating, offensive rating, eFG%, 3P%, 3P rate, offensive rebounds, free throw attempts, FT%, steals and blocks, and lowest defensive rating and turnovers. Green marks the top five, red the bottom five. In matchups, the shaded side has the better number.

Offensive and defensive ratings are points scored and allowed per 100 possessions; net rating is the difference. Possessions are estimated as FGA + 0.44 × FTA − OREB + TOV, averaged between the two teams, so values can differ slightly from NBA.com's.

eFG% = (FGM + 0.5 × 3PM) ÷ FGA, 3P% = 3PM ÷ 3PA, 3P rate = 3PA ÷ FGA (the share of shots that are threes), and FT% = FTM ÷ FTA, all totaled across the window. OREB, FTA, TOV, STL and BLK are per-game averages. Regular season only; a team with fewer games than the window uses all of its games. Game times are in your local time zone. Records and betting lines (spread, over/under and moneyline, from the sportsbook ESPN lists, usually DraftKings) are refreshed each morning and again mid-afternoon Eastern, so they are snapshots, not live odds; a game shows "Line not posted yet" until one is available. Source: ESPN box scores, updated every morning.
